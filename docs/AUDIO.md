# Donkey roulette audio

The default recording was supplied for this project as `ruleta dedsafio 1 test  con sonido en edicion - Sir  DDR.mp3`.

Embedded source metadata credits **Sir | DDR**, with the title **ruleta dedsafio 1 test | con sonido en edicion**. The audio is a supplied third-party asset; the code's MIT license does not establish ownership of the original recording.

The 48 kHz stereo recording was converted to Ogg Vorbis quality 5. Its final trailing silence was trimmed from 8.28 to 8.00 seconds. It is included at `assets/purefix/sounds/donkey_spin.ogg` and referenced by the client-local `purefix:donkey.spin` sound event. No server resource pack is needed. The original MP3 is not included in the project archive.

At 1x the recording takes eight seconds; at 2x it takes four seconds with a higher pitch. Classic sound mode uses vanilla Minecraft clicks and XP/level-up effects instead.
